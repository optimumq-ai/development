Massachusetts City Public Records Request — Process Flow

Email-based request for copies · Massachusetts Public Records Law (M.G.L. c. 66, §§ 6A, 10 & 10A; definition at M.G.L. c. 4, § 7, cl. 26; regulations at 950 CMR 32.00) · Requester shown beyond the left margin · Summary level, July 2026 · Not legal advice
Governing clock: MA Public Records Law: email to city RAO OK. Written response in 10 business days; municipal production by 25 business days; Supervisor of Records can add up to 30 more (single extension). Miss the 10-day response = appealable non-response AND all fees forfeited (s10(e)).
