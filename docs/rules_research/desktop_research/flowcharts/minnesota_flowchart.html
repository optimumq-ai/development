Minnesota City Public Records Request — Process Flow

Email-based request for copies · Minnesota Government Data Practices Act (MGDPA) (Minn. Stat. ch. 13 (esp. secs. 13.03, 13.04, 13.08, 13.085); Minn. R. ch. 1205) · Requester shown beyond the left margin · Summary level, July 2026 · Not legal advice
Governing clock: No fixed clock for public requests: comply in an 'appropriate and prompt manner,' copies 'as soon as reasonably possible' (13.03). Hard 10-business-day clock only for data subjects (13.04). No deemed denial; unreasonable delay = de facto denial per IPAD; one late response can violate (Webster).

In practice the reasonable-time standard flexes for: clarification exchanges, awaiting prepayment of the cost estimate, and volume/complexity (but burden alone does not excuse compliance — Webster litigation).
