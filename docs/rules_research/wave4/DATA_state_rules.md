### Material negatives

- `appeal.administrative.none` — SB270 creates no administrative or AG-review appeal path for a records denial. The only statutory consequence of non-response is the rebuttable constructive-denial presumption (AL-0027/0028/0036/0037), which positions the requester to seek judicial relief. Absence of a found administrative appeal is not proof none exists elsewhere.
- `appeal.judicial.none` — The Act does not itself prescribe a judicial appeal/enforcement procedure, timeline, or venue. Enforcement historically proceeds by mandamus in circuit court under prior Alabama case law (e.g., the Open Records Act line of cases); that remedy/procedure was intentionally excluded here under the inclusion test (remedy/enforcement-amount) except for the deemed-denial trigger. A dedicated case-law pass is needed for the mandamus standard.
- `redaction.segregability` — No explicit statewide segregability / &#x27;redact-and-release the non-exempt remainder&#x27; mandate. Redaction is contemplated only as a processing-time factor in the standard/time-intensive definitions and as preserved by the savings clause (36-12-43(c)); the duty to release segregable portions is not affirmatively stated in SB270.
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide fixed copy rate, per-page ceiling, or itemized fee schedule. Fees are governed only by the &#x27;reasonable fee set by the public officer&#x27; soft standard (AL-0039); each officer/municipality sets the actual amount.
- `fee.waiver_indigency` — No statutory fee-waiver, reduced-fee, or public-interest/indigency provision found in SB270.
- `records.format_electronic` — No explicit rule governing electronic-record format, native format, or a requester&#x27;s right to choose format. The Act speaks to copies and inspection generally without a format-selection provision.
- `custody.preservation_hold` — No request-triggered or litigation-triggered preservation/legal-hold obligation is created by SB270 (the litigation provision, 36-12-46 / AL-0052, bars using the Act in lieu of discovery rather than imposing a hold). Routine retention schedules are governed separately and excluded.
- `intake.acknowledgment_content` — The statute requires an acknowledgment (AL-0024/0030) but does not prescribe required content or form for the acknowledgment itself.

### Structural branches

- `classification.standard_request_threshold` — Two-track processing fork: requests estimated at &lt;8 staff hours run the standard track (AL-0009).
- `classification.time_intensive_threshold` — Requests estimated at &gt;8 staff hours run the separate time-intensive track with its own longer timeline (AL-0010).
- `classification.time_intensive_withdrawal_option` — After the time-intensive designation notice, the request branches: requester may withdraw and resubmit a non-time-intensive request, or elect to proceed (which starts the 45-business-day clock) (AL-0032).
- `clarification.tolling_restart` — Seeking clarification/additional information tolls all timelines and restarts them from zero on receipt, as if a new request (AL-0023).
- `coverage.excluded_public_bodies` — Judicial branch and Article VI constitutional offices are routed out of the 36-12-43 through 36-12-45 procedural machinery (AL-0006).
- `redaction.critical_infrastructure_owner_notice` — Requests touching critical infrastructure trigger a mandatory written notice-and-comment branch to the infrastructure owner before disposition (AL-0047).
- `custody.custodian_referral` — Non-existent-record denials may branch to a referral identifying the proper custodian/location (AL-0049).
- `intake.default_delivery_method` — Absent adopted written procedures, the engine falls back to a statutory default submission path (hand/mail to main office) and receipt-determination rules (AL-0017, AL-0018).
- `denials.constructive_denial_exceptions` — Deemed-denial presumption can be suspended indefinitely when the officer has responded in part, reached agreement, is negotiating, or has reasonably communicated status (AL-0029, AL-0038).
- `tracking.time_intensive_log` — Designating a request time-intensive requires creating an entry in a confidential pending-request log (AL-0035).
- `payment.requester_opt_out` — A standard request enters a terminal abandoned state if the requester opts not to pay the estimated fee (AL-0042).

### Material negatives

- `deadline.acknowledgment_window` — No separate acknowledgment/receipt step distinct from the substantive response. NRS 239.0107(1) sets a single 5-business-day window within which the entity must produce, refer, extend, or deny; there is no earlier statutory duty merely to acknowledge receipt.
- `payment.deposit_advance` — No statutory authority in Chapter 239 for an advance deposit or prepayment before search/production of ordinary copies. Fee tender is expressly required only for certified copies (NRS 239.030, NV-0018).
- `fee.commercial_purpose_distinction` — Chapter 239 draws no distinction between commercial and non-commercial requesters and creates no purpose-based fee tier; the actual-cost ceiling (NRS 239.052) applies without regard to requester purpose.
- `fee.personnel_labor_charge` — No fee is authorized for staff time, labor, or overhead. The &#x27;actual cost&#x27; definition (NRS 239.005(1)) excludes costs incurred regardless of a request, and 2019 SB287 repealed the former extraordinary-use-of-personnel fee (NRS 239.055). Absence of a labor charge is affirmative, not merely unfound.
- `enforcement.deemed_denial_clock` — No fixed constructive/deemed-denial trigger (no &#x27;silence for N days = denial&#x27;). The only silence-related trigger is the soft &#x27;unreasonably delayed&#x27; standard for a district-court application under NRS 239.011(1) (NV-0028).
- `appeal.administrative.internal_stage` — No internal or administrative appeal stage exists in Chapter 239; the exclusive review mechanism is a district-court application (NRS 239.011). Emitted as a negative so it does not falsely merge with other states&#x27; internal-appeal stages.
- `deadline.time_computation` — Chapter 239 does not define &#x27;business day&#x27; or &#x27;working day&#x27;, nor does it provide tolling, holiday-exclusion, or day-counting rules for the 5-business-day (NRS 239.0107) or 3-working-day (NRS 239.015(2)) periods. Later pass may need general NRS time-computation rules or municipal practice.
- `clarification.nonresponse_abandonment` — No provision deeming a request abandoned or closed if the requester fails to respond to a narrowing/clarification effort. NRS 239.0107(1)(c)(2) imposes a duty to assist but attaches no requester-side deadline or abandonment consequence.
- `preservation.litigation_hold` — Chapter 239 contains no preservation/litigation hold triggered by the making of a records request or by an appeal on that request. (General records-retention schedules exist under NRS 239.051-239.125 but are routine retention, excluded.)

### Structural branches

- `deadline.extension_notice` — NRS 239.0107(1)(c)(1): a required written notice, given when the entity cannot produce by day five, declaring the earliest reasonably-believed availability date — a distinct processing stage that pauses production against a self-set soft date (NV-0008).
- `communications.availability_reexplanation` — NRS 239.0107(1)(c)(1): a recurring follow-up written explanation plus a new availability date whenever a previously represented date lapses (NV-0009).
- `clarification.narrowing_assistance` — NRS 239.0107(1)(c)(2): a mandatory &#x27;reasonable effort&#x27; to help the requester focus the request when production cannot occur by day five (NV-0010).
- `routing.no_custody_referral` — NRS 239.0107(1)(b): a referral branch — written notice of no custody plus name/address of the entity that has the record (NV-0011).
- `denial.written_notice_with_citation` — NRS 239.0107(1)(d): the confidential-denial branch requiring written notice with a specific legal citation (NV-0012).
- `redaction.segregability_required` — NRS 239.010(3): mandatory partial-disclosure/redaction path barring denial of a record that mixes confidential and non-confidential content (NV-0013).
- `production.electronic_format_on_request` — NRS 239.010(4): electronic-format production branch with its four statutory exceptions (NV-0014).
- `appeal.judicial.district_court_application` — NRS 239.011(1): the sole external review path — district-court application on denial, unreasonable delay, or fee dispute; Nevada has no internal administrative appeal stage (NV-0028).
- `appeal.judicial.thirty_year_confidential_records` — NRS 239.0115: a separate district-court path to reach records held 30+ years, carrying a rebuttable presumption of access and enumerated exceptions (NV-0029).
- `fee.veterans_affairs_exemption` — NRS 239.020: a mandatory free-certified-copy branch for federal VA benefit-eligibility determinations (NV-0027).
