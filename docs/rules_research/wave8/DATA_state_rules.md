### Material negatives

- `response.fixed_numeric_deadline` — Wisconsin sets NO fixed numeric response/acknowledgment deadline. The only time-for-compliance standard is the soft &#x27;as soon as practicable and without delay&#x27; (19.35(4)(a); captured as rule WI-0023). There is no separate statutory acknowledgment-letter requirement and no day-count within which a determination must issue.
- `fee.commercial_purpose_surcharge` — No commercial-purpose fee distinction or surcharge exists. Fees are limited to actual/necessary/direct cost regardless of purpose, and records may not be denied merely because the requester has a commercial purpose (58 Atty. Gen. 67). No &#x27;commercial requester&#x27; branch to configure.
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide fixed per-page copy rate. The only constraint is the &#x27;actual, necessary and direct cost of reproduction and transcription&#x27; ceiling (19.35(3)(a), WI-0035); the city/authority supplies the operative per-unit value under that ceiling.
- `fee.inspection_charge` — No fee is authorized for mere inspection. The four permitted fee tasks are reproduction/transcription, photographing/processing, locating (only if $50+), and mailing/shipping (19.35(3)); inspection itself carries no charge.
- `clarification.mandatory_clarification_clock` — No statutory clarification clock or mandatory duty to help a requester narrow an overbroad request. A custodian need not take affirmative steps to limit a search and may (at some point) reject an insufficiently limited request (Gehl v. Connors; Schopper v. Gehring). Sufficiency is judged by the soft &#x27;reasonably describes&#x27; standard (WI-0011).
- `format.electronic_native_format_mandate` — No general statutory right to a record in a requester-specified electronic/native format or to direct access to an authority&#x27;s databases; PDF satisfied an &#x27;electronic/digital&#x27; request and requesters are not entitled to query databases directly (WIREdata v. Village of Sussex, 2008 WI 69). Format rights are limited to the medium-specific provisions in 19.35(1)(c)-(f).
- `appeal.administrative_tribunal` — No formal internal administrative-appeal tribunal or records ombudsman with binding authority. Review is via mandamus (court) or application to the AG/DA (19.37(1)); the AG may also give non-binding advisory interpretations under 19.39.
- `payment.deposit_schedule` — No deposit regime beyond the single prepayment rule (prepayment permitted when total fees exceed $5, 19.35(3)(f), WI-0042). No tiered deposit schedule or escrow requirement is specified.

### Structural branches

- `notice.record_subject_pre_release` — Woznicki record-subject pre-release notice path (19.356(2)-(5)): 3-day notice to subject, subject&#x27;s 5-day intent and 10-day restraint-action windows, and a 12-day (plus pendency) release hold. A WI-specific reverse-review branch the engine must execute before releasing the three enumerated record categories.
- `notice.public_official_record_augmentation` — Public-office record-subject augmentation path (19.356(9)): 3-day notice then a 5-day window for the subject to augment the record with written comments/documentation, with mandatory release as augmented.
- `special_records.self_access_determination_sequence` — 19.35(4)(c) ordered determination sequence for identified self-access requests: evaluate sub.(1)(a) first, grant if a right exists, otherwise evaluate sub.(1)(am). A distinct decision fork not present for general requests.
- `preservation.destruction_hold` — Request- and litigation-triggered destruction-hold states (19.35(5)): the record enters a preservation state on receipt of the request (until grant or 60/90-day post-denial period) and a further hold on written notice of a s. 19.37 action.
- `routing.it_custodian_passthrough` — Local-IT-authority custodian passthrough (19.35(7)): a request to a local IT authority storing law-enforcement records is not answered by it; it must deny the local-law-enforcement portion and the owning authority is the custodian.
- `appeal.enforcement_paths` — Two-track enforcement fork (19.37(1)): requester&#x27;s own mandamus action (judicial) and/or written application to the AG or DA (administrative), each with distinct remedies; a written request is a prerequisite to either.

### Material negatives

- `deadlines.final_completion_deadline` — Oregon sets NO fixed numeric outer deadline to complete a response. ORS 192.329(1) requires completion &#x27;as soon as practicable and without unreasonable delay&#x27; (soft), and the only hard 10-business-day duty (ORS 192.329(5)) is satisfied by a status statement with a self-set estimated date, not actual completion.
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide fixed per-page copy rate or per-hour labor rate. Fees are bounded only by the soft &#x27;reasonably calculated to reimburse actual cost&#x27; standard (ORS 192.324(4)(a)); each city sets its own rates in its published procedure.
- `preservation.request_triggered_hold` — No express provision in ORS 192.311-192.478 imposing a litigation/appeal- or request-triggered preservation hold on records subject to a pending request. Routine retention schedules live in ORS ch. 192/166 archives provisions and are out of scope. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `payment.advance_deposit` — No statute expressly authorizes or requires advance deposits/prepayment beyond the ORS 192.324(4)(c) $25 written-estimate-and-confirmation gate and the ORS 192.329(3) fee-payment suspension. Deposit practice, if any, is local.
- `intake.request_tracking_number` — No statutory requirement that a public body assign or provide a tracking number/reference for a request. Not legally required; excluded from primary rules.
- `intake.acknowledgment_method` — Statute requires a written acknowledgment/response content but does not prescribe a delivery channel (mail/email/portal); channel is unspecified by statute.

### Structural branches

- `intake.written_procedure_published` — Each public body must publish a written request procedure naming designated recipient(s) with addresses and fee-calculation method (ORS 192.324(7)) — the intake/routing entry point.
- `communications.acknowledgment_content` — Acknowledgment must make a custody determination (custodian / not custodian / uncertain), a three-way branch that drives routing (ORS 192.324(2)).
- `deadlines.impracticability_exception` — Oregon-specific suspension of the 5- and 10-business-day deadlines for staff unavailability, service impediment, or request volume, reverting to a soft standard (ORS 192.329(6)-(8)).
- `appeal.administrative.petition_da_local_body` — Municipal/non-state denials are reviewed by the county district attorney (not the AG), with suit in that county&#x27;s circuit court (ORS 192.415).
- `appeal.judicial.elected_official_direct_to_court` — Records held by, or withholding-claimed by, an elected official bypass administrative AG/DA review and go directly to circuit court (ORS 192.427) — a distinct Oregon path.
- `appeal.administrative.deemed_denial_on_inaction` — AG/DA silence for 7 days is a deemed denial that unlocks the judicial stage (ORS 192.418(1)).
- `denials.deemed_denial_elected_official` — Elected-official silence for 7 days is a deemed denial unlocking direct judicial relief (ORS 192.418(2)).
- `payment.fee_suspends_response` — A stated fee tolls the completion obligation until paid/waived, feeding the 60-day nonpayment close (ORS 192.329(3)).
- `clarification.request_suspends_response` — A good-faith clarification request tolls the completion obligation until the requester responds or declines, feeding the 60-day clarification close (ORS 192.329(4)).

### Material negatives

- `deadlines.universal_acknowledgment_window` — No fixed universal acknowledgment deadline for ordinary requests. The 5-business-day 44:32(D) window applies only when the custodian QUESTIONS whether an item is a public record; the general obligation is functionally enforced by the 5-business-day constructive-denial trigger (44:35(A), LA-0030). Absence of a found universal-acknowledgment statute is not proof none exists.
- `appeal.administrative.review_stage` — No administrative-appeal stage exists in the Public Records Law. There is no AG/ombudsman/records-authority binding review; the Attorney General issues only advisory opinions. Enforcement is by direct suit in district court (44:35). Emitted as a negative so the cross-state join does not manufacture a phantom administrative stage.
- `preservation.request_triggered_hold` — No request- or litigation-triggered preservation/hold provision in the PRL. La. R.S. 44:36 imposes only a routine default 3-year retention (excluded as routine retention). No statute directs a custodian to freeze destruction because a request, appeal, or suit is pending.
- `fee.copy_rate_statutory_cap_local` — No statewide statutory per-page or per-item copy-fee CAP for municipal/local custodians; the only constraint is &#x27;reasonable&#x27; (44:32(C)(1)(a), LA-0021). A uniform dollar schedule exists only for STATE agencies (44:32(C)(2)(a), LA-0026).
- `clarification.response_clock` — No deadline governs the clarification/narrowing dialogue. The narrowing step under 44:32(A)(2) (LA-0034) is mandatory before a disruption-based denial but carries no statutory clock.
- `format.requester_format_preference` — No statutory right to receive records in a requester-specified format. The custodian must provide copies and MAY transmit electronic copies (44:32(C)(1)(a), LA-0020), but no format-preference or native-format mandate is stated.
- `intake.request_tracking_number` — No statutory requirement to assign or communicate a tracking/reference number, or to maintain a public request log.
- `deadlines.production_outer_limit` — No absolute outer deadline for completing production of a voluminous request; the statute relies on the &#x27;time reasonably necessary for collection, segregation, redaction, examination, or review&#x27; estimate (44:35(A), LA-0031) rather than a fixed maximum.

### Structural branches

- `production.immediate_vs_active_use` — 44:33(B) forks on record availability: immediately-available records are presented immediately (LA-0015); records in active use route to a written certificate fixing a day/hour within 3 business days (LA-0016).
- `custody.record_not_in_custody_certification` — 44:34 branch: when the record is not in the applied-to person&#x27;s custody/control, the request routes to a written certification identifying reason, location, and the correct custodian (LA-0018).
- `denials.status_determination_path` — 44:32(D) branch: when the custodian questions public-record status, the request enters a 5-business-day written-determination-with-legal-basis state (LA-0028/LA-0029).
- `denials.constructive_denial_path` — 44:35(A) terminal branch: 5 business days without a written determination or a time estimate moves the request into a constructive-denial state that unlocks judicial enforcement (LA-0030).
- `deadlines.time_estimate_path` — 44:35(A) alternative branch: issuing a reasonable-time estimate diverts the request off the constructive-denial track into an extended-processing state (LA-0031).
- `denials.substantial_disruption_path` — 44:32(A)(2) branch: a request deemed to substantially disrupt operations must pass through a mandatory narrow/specify dialogue before a discretionary denial (LA-0034).
- `appeal.judicial.enforcement_path` — 44:35 judicial path (no administrative predicate): district-court mandamus/injunctive/declaratory action, venued in the custodian&#x27;s parish and tried by preference/summarily (LA-0032/LA-0033).
- `payment.hold_for_payment_path` — 44:32(C)(1)(a) branch: nonpayment after advance notice, or an outstanding prior balance, holds production pending payment (LA-0023/LA-0024); after-hours examination routes to an overtime-compensation-prepaid path (LA-0027).
