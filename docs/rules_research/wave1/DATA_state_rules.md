### Material negatives (things the law is silent on / does *not* impose)

- `appeal.administrative` — No statutory administrative appeal or AG-review stage exists in the CPRA. Enforcement is exclusively judicial (§ 7923.000). Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists in local ordinance, but the state statute provides none.
- `denial.deemed_constructive` — No explicit statutory &#x27;deemed denial&#x27; / constructive-denial clock (e.g., &#x27;silence for N days = denial&#x27;). Failure to meet the 10-day determination duty exposes the agency to judicial enforcement but the statute does not convert silence into an automatic denial with a terminal clock.
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide fixed per-page copy rate. The statute caps fees at &#x27;direct costs of duplication&#x27; (§ 7922.530(a)); the city supplies the actual value under that ceiling. (Some jurisdictions and courts treat ~$0.10-0.25/page as within direct cost, but that is not a statewide statutory number.)
- `intake.form_requirement` — No statutory required request form, and no requirement that a request be in writing to trigger the access right (inspection may be requested orally). A WRITTEN request is what triggers the written-denial duties of § 7922.540 — but writing is not a precondition to access itself.
- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No separate mandatory acknowledgment deadline distinct from the 10-day determination. The 10-day determination under § 7922.535(a) is the only intake-side statutory clock.
- `payment.deposit` — No statutory advance-deposit or fee-estimate-and-hold scheme for ordinary requests; the statute conditions copy release on &#x27;payment of fees covering direct costs of duplication&#x27; (§ 7922.530(a)) but does not authorize a deposit process.
- `clarification.clock_toll` — No explicit statutory tolling or pausing of the 10-day determination clock while the agency seeks clarification. The assistance duty (§ 7922.600) exists but carries no attached toll of the § 7922.535 deadline.
- `deadline.production_numeric` — No numeric statutory deadline for actual PRODUCTION of records; production is governed by the soft &#x27;promptly&#x27; standard (§ 7922.530(a)) and the agency&#x27;s self-set &#x27;estimated date&#x27; (§ 7922.535(a)), not a fixed day-count.

### Material negatives (things the law is silent on / does *not* impose)

- `deadline.acknowledgment_window` — Texas imposes NO fixed statutory acknowledgment or final-response day-count. The only response duty is to &#x27;promptly produce&#x27; (soft standard, § 552.221(a)-(b)); the sole hard 10-business-day event is a certification duty when records cannot be produced in that time (§ 552.221(d)), not a decision/production deadline. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `appeal.administrative.requestor_appeal` — There is no requestor-initiated administrative appeal of a denial. The Act instead compels the governmental body to seek an AG ruling to withhold (§ 552.301); a requestor&#x27;s recourse for noncompliance is a complaint to the AG or a mandamus suit under § 552.321 (external litigation, excluded).
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide per-page copy rate is fixed in the statute itself; the concrete rates/ceilings are set by attorney general cost rules (1 Tex. Admin. Code ch. 70), which were not opened this pass. § 552.262 only supplies the ceiling mechanism. Verify the current TAC rate schedule in a later pass.
- `deadline.time_calculation` — The Act repeatedly uses &#x27;business day&#x27; but a general statutory definition/holiday-exclusion rule for computing the PIA clocks was not separately captured this pass; confirm how &#x27;business day&#x27; and skip-days are defined for §§ 552.221/552.301.
- Preservation/litigation-hold triggered by the request itself: no distinct PIA preservation-hold provision was located this pass (routine retention schedules are out of scope); flag for the retention/records-authority pass.

### Material negatives (things the law is silent on / does *not* impose)

- `clarification.cure_period` — No explicit statutory clarification/cure clock. FOIL&#x27;s only intake-adequacy standard is that a record be &#x27;reasonably described&#x27; (§ 89(3)(a)); there is no statutory deadline for a requester to narrow or clarify a request. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists (COOG guidance encourages agencies to assist).
- `clarification.nonresponse_abandonment` — No statutory abandonment/closure trigger for requester non-response (e.g., failure to pay or clarify). Statute conditions production on &#x27;payment of, or offer to pay&#x27; (§ 89(3)(a)) but sets no time limit after which a request is deemed abandoned.
- `payment.deposit_threshold` — No statutory advance-deposit percentage or dollar threshold in Article 6. Production is gated on &#x27;payment of, or offer to pay&#x27; the prescribed fee (§ 89(3)(a)); no formula (e.g., &#x27;50% deposit if over $X&#x27;) is provided by statute.
- `deadlines.final_production_hard_deadline` — No fixed numeric outer deadline for actual production. After the 5-business-day response and the 20-business-day checkpoint, the operative standard is a &#x27;date certain within a reasonable period&#x27; (§ 89(3)(a)) — a soft standard, not a hard statewide cap.
- `fee.fixed_statewide_rate` — There is no fixed statewide per-page fee. The 25 cents/page figure is a CEILING (§ 87(1)(b)(iii)); municipalities set their own per-page fee up to that cap, and no statutory floor exists. (Captured affirmatively as NY-0024.)
- `production.rolling_installments` — No statutory requirement or prohibition governing rolling/installment production of voluminous requests; the statute addresses timing via the acknowledgement/date-certain mechanism rather than mandated installments.
- `time.business_day_definition` — Article 6 uses &#x27;business days&#x27; for the 5-, 20-, and 10-day windows but &#x27;days&#x27; (unqualified) for the 30-day appeal-filing window (§ 89(4)(a)); the article does not itself define how business days are counted (e.g., holidays).
- EXCLUDED — § 89(4)(c) attorney&#x27;s fees and litigation costs (&#x27;may assess&#x27; / &#x27;shall assess&#x27; where petitioner &#x27;substantially prevailed&#x27; and agency &#x27;had no reasonable basis&#x27;): a Remedy on the Remove list. The linked constructive-denial trigger it references is captured at NY-0013/NY-0040.
- EXCLUDED — § 89(4)(d) and § 89(5)(d) appellate procedure (preference, 60-day abandonment, CPLR 5513 appeals): litigation procedure, not request-handling behavior.
- EXCLUDED — § 89(4)(b) / § 89(5)(f) burden of proof in an Article 78 proceeding (agency must prove exemption): litigation burden; the operational duty to give particularized justification is captured at NY-0034.
- EXCLUDED — § 87(3)(a)-(b) duty to maintain a final-vote record and an employee name/title/salary roster, and § 87(3)(c) annually-updated subject-matter list: general recordkeeping obligations (Remove list), not per-request processing.
- EXCLUDED — § 89(8) criminal violation for willful concealment/destruction of a record: a penalty provision (Remove list).
- EXCLUDED — § 89(7) non-disclosure of home addresses of officers/employees/retirees/applicants: an exemption (special record content), not a processing step; belongs to the exemption catalog rather than the workflow.
- SCOPE FLAG (not excluded, but municipally inapplicable) — § 89(3)(c) online-submission duty and § 87(3)(c)/§ 87(4)(c) website-posting duties apply to &#x27;state agency&#x27; only, so they do not bind ordinary municipalities. Municipal platforms should treat these as state-agency configuration.

### Material negatives (things the law is silent on / does *not* impose)

- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No separate acknowledgment-of-receipt deadline distinct from the substantive response. Illinois folds acknowledgment into the 5-business-day comply-or-deny window (Section 3(d)); there is no statutory duty to send a bare receipt confirmation on a shorter clock. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `clarification.ambiguity_clock` — No general clarification clock that tolls or restarts the response deadline when a request is ambiguous. The only statutory clarification mechanism is the confer-to-narrow duty for an unduly-burdensome categorical request (IL-0016), which does not itself pause the clock.
- `request.tracking_log_public` — No statutory requirement that municipalities maintain or publish a public request-tracking log. Section 3.5 requires the FOIA officer to note the receipt date and compute the due date internally, but this is an internal notation, not a mandated tracking system.
- `appeal.fee` — No filing fee is imposed for a request for review to the Public Access Counselor; the administrative-review path is free to the requester. Stated as an absence of any fee provision in Section 9.5.
- `intake.language_access` — No explicit statutory translation or language-access requirement for processing a request within FOIA (5 ILCS 140). Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `fee.deposit_threshold` — No general statutory deposit threshold (e.g., prepay when estimate exceeds $X) for ordinary requests. Full-prepayment-before-copying is permitted only on commercial (Section 3.1) and recurrent-requester (Section 3.2) estimates (IL-0019); no analogous deposit rule was found for standard requests.

### Material negatives (things the law is silent on / does *not* impose)

- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — VFOIA has no acknowledgment step distinct from the substantive response. The five-working-day response IS the first required communication (§ 2.2-3704(B)); there is no separate &#x27;we received your request&#x27; clock. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `appeal.administrative.internal_appeal` — VFOIA provides NO internal administrative appeal and no binding agency/AG review of a denial. The only formal remedy is the judicial mandamus/injunction petition (§ 2.2-3713). The Virginia FOIA Advisory Council (§ 30-179) issues only nonbinding advisory opinions. Emit no appeal.administrative.* rule for Virginia.
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide fixed or ceiling per-page copy rate. Fees are bounded only by the &#x27;actual cost&#x27; ceiling (VA-0021, VA-0024); the per-page amount, if any, is set by each locality under that ceiling.
- `fee.commercial_purpose_surcharge` — VFOIA draws no commercial-vs-noncommercial requester distinction and authorizes no commercial-purpose fee surcharge; &#x27;commercial purpose&#x27; is not a defined term in § 2.2-3701. Charges turn on actual cost regardless of requester purpose.
- `eligibility.any_person` — Deliberately NOT emitted as a rule: Virginia does not grant universal any-person access. Standing is restricted to citizens of the Commonwealth plus in-state media (see VA-0001). Emitting the shared any_person/no_residency key here would misjoin Virginia with open-access states.
- `classification.mrr_or_recurring_request` — No statutory classification for multi-record, recurring, or subscription requests, and no distinct processing track for them. Each request runs the single § 2.2-3704 pipeline.
- `preservation.litigation_hold_on_request` — No VFOIA provision imposes a preservation/litigation hold triggered by the request or by an enforcement petition. Records retention runs under the separate Public Records Act (Title 42.1) on routine schedules, which are out of scope. No request-triggered hold rule found.
- `intake.submission_channel_required` — VFOIA prescribes no mandatory submission channel (no required form, portal, or writing). A request need not even cite the chapter (VA-0006); the statute is silent on medium, so no channel rule is emitted.

### Material negatives (things the law is silent on / does *not* impose)

- `response.final_production_deadline` — Washington sets no fixed statutory deadline for FINAL production of records. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `clarification.requester_response_clock` — No statutory numeric period within which a requester must answer a clarification request. RCW 42.56.520(3)(b) states only that if the requester fails to respond and the request is wholly unclear the agency need not respond (WA-0016) — no day-count is fixed.
- `appeal.administrative.requester_filing_deadline` — No statutory deadline for a requester to REQUEST internal review of a denial. The internal-review mechanism (WA-0036) is agency-established and self-completes at the end of the second business day after denial; the statute imposes no filing window on the requester.
- `request.tracking_log_required` — No statute requires municipalities to maintain a request tracking log or assign a tracking number. The AG model rules (WAC 44-14) recommend logging as a best practice, but that is advisory guidance, not a statutory requirement.
- `search.staff_time_chargeable` — No provision authorizes charging for staff search/location time; RCW 42.56.120(1) bars any fee for locating records and RCW 42.56.070(7)(b) excludes staff salaries/overhead from copy costs. Recorded as the absence of a search-fee rule, consistent with WA-0019/WA-0028.
- `intake.acknowledgment_separate_from_response` — Washington has no acknowledgment step separate from the five-business-day response; acknowledgment is itself one of the five permitted first responses (RCW 42.56.520(1)(c)-(d)), so there is no distinct acknowledgment deadline to configure.

### Material negatives (things the law is silent on / does *not* impose)

- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No separate statutory acknowledgment/receipt deadline distinct from substantive response. Georgia&#x27;s three-business-day period (50-18-71(b)(1)(A)) is a production/response ceiling, not a mere acknowledgment obligation; there is no standalone &#x27;acknowledge within N days&#x27; rule.
- `denials.constructive_denial_clock` — No statutory deemed/constructive-denial trigger (no &#x27;silence for N days = denial&#x27;). Georgia enforcement is judicial and turns on noncompliance/no substantial justification (50-18-73), not an automatic terminal clock. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `appeal.administrative.internal_review` — No statutory administrative appeal to a state ombudsman or records authority. Review is judicial (superior court, 50-18-73) plus discretionary Attorney General enforcement; the AG&#x27;s mediation program is informal and non-statutory.
- `clarification.overbroad_request_clock` — No statutory clarification/narrowing clock and no requester-clarification-then-abandonment (nonresponse-abandonment) mechanism in the Open Records Act.
- `tracking.request_log_requirement` — No statewide statutory requirement to maintain a public request log or assign tracking numbers for open records requests.
- `fee.municipal_flat_rate` — No statewide-mandated flat municipal copy or labor rate. The state supplies only ceilings (10 cents/page for letter/legal; lowest-paid qualified employee&#x27;s prorated hourly salary for labor) and a fixed 15-minute free increment; the city fills in the operative values under those ceilings.
- `deadlines.time_calculation_method` — The Act counts in &#x27;business days&#x27; for the core response window but does not codify a general holiday/weekend computation method or a rule for when receipt occurs (e.g., after-hours or weekend submissions); computation details are left unspecified. Manual verification / later AG-opinion pass recommended.

### Material negatives (things the law is silent on / does *not* impose)

- `response.acknowledgment_window` — Ohio sets NO fixed numeric acknowledgment or response deadline. The only processing timing standards are the soft &#x27;promptly&#x27; (inspection) and &#x27;within a reasonable period of time&#x27; (copies) in R.C. 149.43(B)(1), captured as soft-standard rules OH-0007 and OH-0008. No day-count for acknowledgment exists.
- `denials.written_explanation_deadline` — R.C. 149.43(B)(3) requires a written denial explanation for written requests but attaches NO numeric deadline to providing it. Absence of a found day-count is not proof none exists elsewhere.
- `clarification.revise_response_clock` — The opportunity-to-revise duty (R.C. 149.43(B)(2)) carries no statutory clock — no deadline by which the office must offer revision or the requester must respond.
- `enforcement.constructive_denial_daycount` — Ohio has no fixed &#x27;silence for N days = denial&#x27; trigger. A failure to respond within a reasonable time may be treated as a denial for mandamus/Court-of-Claims purposes, but the statute states no numeric constructive-denial day count.
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide statutory per-page copy rate. R.C. 149.43(B)(1) sets only an &#x27;at cost&#x27;/actual-cost ceiling (OH-0018); the city fills in the operative rate. (Older R.C. 149.43(B)(1) practice historically referenced actual cost; no fixed cents-per-page in statute.)
- `payment.deposit_percentage` — R.C. 149.43(B)(6) permits requiring advance payment of the full cost but sets no partial-deposit percentage or threshold above which prepayment may be demanded.
- `custody.wrong_department_forwarding` — No explicit statutory duty to forward a misdirected request to the correct office/custodian. The duty is stated as resting on the office or &#x27;person responsible for public records&#x27; (OH-0013); routing/forwarding is not statutorily prescribed.
- `intake.request_tracking_log` — No statutory requirement to log/track requests or assign tracking numbers for an individual request under R.C. 149.43. (A public-records policy is required at the office level but tracking of individual requests is not mandated.)
