### Material negatives

- `deadline.determination_extension` — KORA sets no fixed extension mechanism. Beyond the three-business-day action deadline (KS-0026), the only &#x27;extension&#x27; path is the custodian&#x27;s &#x27;detailed explanation of the cause for further delay&#x27; with an earliest date of availability (KS-0027) — a soft standard, not a numeric extension with enumerated grounds.
- `denial.deemed_denial_on_nonresponse` — No explicit statutory &#x27;silence = denial / deemed denial&#x27; trigger. KORA imposes the three-business-day action deadline and provides judicial enforcement, but does not state that non-response after N days constitutes a denial. (The 2025 fee-mitigation provision creates a deemed WITHDRAWAL for requester non-response — KS-0042 — which is not a deemed denial.)
- `appeal.administrative.review_right` — No general internal/administrative appeal stage for municipal requests. The only administrative-review path in KORA is a fee-reasonableness appeal to the Secretary of Administration under K.S.A. 45-219(d), and that is limited to records within the state EXECUTIVE BRANCH — inapplicable to municipal requests. Municipal recourse is judicial (K.S.A. 45-222).
- `fee.per_page_rate_ceiling` — No statewide fixed per-page copy rate for general municipal records. Copy fees are governed by &#x27;reasonable&#x27; and &#x27;actual cost&#x27; standards (KS-0033, KS-0034), with the city supplying the operative value; there is no statutory cents-per-page cap in the open records act.
- `fee.waiver_public_interest` — KORA contains no public-interest or indigency fee-waiver provision.
- `fee.commercial_purpose_rate` — No separate commercial-purpose fee tier. KORA does not define &#x27;commercial purpose&#x27; or set a higher rate for commercial requesters; it instead restricts sale/use of name-and-address lists (K.S.A. 45-230) and permits a non-sale certification (KS-0010).
- `fee.inspection_no_charge` — No explicit statutory rule that inspection is free of charge. K.S.A. 45-218(f) permits charging a fee for &#x27;providing access to&#x27; records; inspection during office hours is provided but the act does not expressly bar an access fee for inspection.
- `preservation.hold_pending_request` — KORA contains no request-triggered preservation or litigation-hold provision; records retention/destruction is governed outside the open records act.
- `clarification.request_permitted` — No express clarification-request mechanism or clarification clock. The nearest provisions are the requirement that a request contain enough information to ascertain the records sought and the &#x27;impossible to determine the records&#x27; technicality exception (KS-0008), plus the 2025 cost-mitigation interactive communication (KS-0041).
- `intake.oral_request_permitted` — Oral requests are not guaranteed; the agency &#x27;may require a written request&#x27; (KS-0004). No statutory duty to accept oral requests.
- `production.electronic_format_choice` — No general statutory requester right to choose the electronic format/medium. The duty established judicially (KS-0046) is to provide a copy in the format the agency MAINTAINS the record, and the agency need not allow requester devices to be attached (KS-0047).

### Structural branches

- `withdrawal.fee_mitigation_nonresponse` — Deemed-withdrawal state (KS-0042): after a triggered cost-mitigation contact (KS-0041), requester silence for three business days moves the request into a &#x27;deemed withdrawn&#x27; state, reversible only on subsequent requester contact. A Kansas-specific path added by L. 2025, ch. 89.
- `fee.cost_mitigation_conference_threshold` — Interactive cost-mitigation conference stage (KS-0041): crossing the 5-hour / $200 staff-time threshold forces a mandatory contact-and-confer step before the request proceeds.
- `denial.written_statement_on_request` — Two-step denial path (KS-0028 to KS-0030): denial itself is communicated within the general 3-business-day window, but the written, provision-citing statement of grounds is owed only &#x27;upon request,&#x27; and its own separate 3-business-day clock runs from receipt of that follow-up request.
- `certification.access_or_nonsale_when_restricted` — Restricted-access certification gate (KS-0010): where access or use is limited under K.S.A. 45-221 or 45-230, the request branches to require a written right-of-access or non-sale certification before production.
- `routing.record_type_originating_agency` — Record-type routing forks (KS-0016 to KS-0019): criminal-justice-repository records, asset-seizure-repository records, &#x27;files&#x27; under K.S.A. 75-4379, and ALPR data are each statutorily routed to the originating/owning agency rather than the receiving agency.
- `custody.wrong_recipient_routing` — Non-custodian redirect (KS-0013): a non-custodian recipient must route the requester to the named custodian&#x27;s location.
- `appeal.judicial.enforcement_right` — Judicial enforcement path (KS-0048 to KS-0050): the sole review route for municipal requests is a de novo district-court action with in camera inspection and the burden of proof on the agency; there is no municipal administrative-appeal stage.

### Material negatives

- `deadline.initial_response_window` — Indiana sets NO fixed statutory production/response deadline. The only statutory clocks are the § 5-14-3-9 deemed-denial windows (24 hours / 7 days) and a soft &#x27;reasonable time&#x27; standard for production. Absence of a found fixed window is not proof none exists in a special-record statute.
- `communication.acknowledgment_response_duty` — No statutory requirement that the agency send an acknowledgment or an estimated completion date. (The deemed-denial windows substitute for an affirmative-acknowledgment duty.)
- `clarification.request_permitted` — No explicit statutory clarification procedure or clarification clock; &#x27;reasonable particularity&#x27; gates the duty but the Act does not codify a confer-to-narrow step or a nonresponse-abandonment clock.
- `deadline.determination_extension` — No statutory extension/unusual-circumstances mechanism to lengthen a response window (there is no fixed window to extend).
- `custody.foia_officer_designation` — APRA does not require agencies to designate or publicly identify a public-records/FOIA officer; it only requires a written denial to name the person responsible for the denial (IN-0020).
- `withdrawal.abandonment_fail_inspect_or_pay` — No statutory abandonment/withdrawal rule for a requester who fails to inspect, pay, or respond to a cost estimate.
- `payment.advance_deposit_before_production` — No explicit statutory authorization to require a deposit or advance full payment before producing standard copies (agencies may set fee schedules; advance-payment practice was not found in statute).
- `fee.no_search_review_labor_overhead` — Statute caps copy charges at per-page/actual cost and bars any inspection fee, but does not expressly address (bar or allow) search/review/labor charges for standard copies; treat as unresolved rather than assume either way.
- `production.no_duty_create_new_record` — No express statutory &#x27;no duty to create a new record&#x27; provision found (contrast the reprogramming provisions in § 5-14-3-6, which address separation, not creation).
- `deadline.tolling_pending_requester_response` — No statutory tolling of a response clock while awaiting a requester&#x27;s answer to clarification/estimate (consistent with there being no fixed response clock). Any tolling of the 30-day PAC-complaint window during an informal inquiry (IC 5-14-5) was not captured verbatim and is flagged for a later pass.

### Structural branches

- `appeal.administrative.pac_complaint_right` — Public Access Counselor formal-complaint path (IC 5-14-5): requester files a complaint within 30 days; PAC issues a non-binding advisory opinion within 30 days. A distinct administrative-review stage the engine must express.
- `appeal.judicial.enforcement_right` — Court action under IC 5-14-3-9(e) in the circuit/superior court of the county of denial to compel access; reviewed de novo with the burden on the agency.
- `denial.deemed_denial_on_nonresponse` — Constructive denial: request moves to a denied state when the applicable 24-hour (in-person/phone/enhanced-access) or 7-day (mail/fax) window elapses - a channel-dependent terminal branch.
- `redaction.segregability` — When a record is mixed, the agency must separate disclosable material and produce it; may branch to a reprogramming-cost charge for electronic separation (IC 5-14-3-6).
- `production.enhanced_access_permission` — Optional enhanced-access path via a section-3.5 contract with its own contract-set fee - a fork the engine must support but need not require.
- `classification.presumption_of_openness` — Liberal-construction / burden-on-agency default (IC 5-14-3-1) that governs how ambiguous classification and denial decisions resolve.

### Material negatives

- `classification.deemed_denial` — MA has no automatic &#x27;silence = denial&#x27; or &#x27;silence = grant&#x27; deeming provision for a municipality&#x27;s non-response. Instead, failure to respond within the required timeframe opens the administrative-appeal path (petition to the Supervisor within 90 calendar days of the request, 950 CMR 32.08(1)(e)) and enables judicial review upon the Supervisor&#x27;s failure to issue a timely determination (M.G.L. c. 66, s.10A(a)). Absence of a found deeming rule is not proof none exists.
- `preservation.litigation_hold_trigger` — No explicit statutory preservation/litigation-hold triggered by the request or by an appeal on that request was found in c. 66 s.10/s.10A or 950 CMR 32.00. Records retention is governed separately (records-retention schedules, out of scope under the inclusion test).
- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — MA provides no separate &#x27;acknowledgment&#x27; step distinct from substantive action; the s.10(b) written response (which confirms receipt) and the s.10(a) production duty both run on the same 10-business-day clock. Acknowledgment is folded into MA-0016/MA-0018 rather than being an independent deadline.
- `fee.electronic_record_rate` — No fixed statewide per-unit fee for electronic records; electronic production is governed by the general &#x27;actual cost of reproducing&#x27; ceiling (MA-0029) plus the labor-rate structure. No separate electronic-record rate parameter exists to configure.
- `request.tracking_number_requirement` — No statute or regulation found requiring municipalities to assign or maintain a tracking/reference number for individual requests. (The Supervisor&#x27;s own appeal intake is time-stamped under 950 CMR 32.08(1)(j), but that governs the Division, not the municipal RAO.)
- `fee.deposit_or_prepayment_threshold` — MA sets no numeric deposit threshold triggering mandatory prepayment; the RAO &#x27;may delay provision of records until all fees ... are paid in full&#x27; (MA-0044) but no dollar threshold or advance-deposit percentage is specified to configure.

### Structural branches

- `response.written_response_contents` — The M.G.L. c. 66 s.10(b) written response is a mandatory branch triggered when the entity cannot fully produce within the initial timeframe or intends to withhold; it carries nine required content elements (MA-0019).
- `extension.petition_deadline` — RAO petition to the Supervisor of Records for a single time extension - a bespoke MA stage inserted between the initial response and production, gated by a 20-business-day filing window and a 5-business-day Supervisor decision (MA-0023, MA-0025/26, MA-0027).
- `extension.frivolous_series_relief` — Supervisor may relieve the entity of its obligation to provide copies where a contemporaneous request series is frivolous/harassing - a terminal path ending the request (MA-0028).
- `fee.supervisor_petition_excess` — Entity petition to the Supervisor to charge for redaction time or (municipality) to exceed $25/hr - a MA-specific fee-approval branch with its own 5-business-day decision clock (MA-0037).
- `appeal.administrative.petition_to_supervisor` — Administrative appeal to the Supervisor of Records (Division of Public Records) - MA&#x27;s internal review stage before court, with 90-day filing windows and a 10-business-day determination (MA-0057 through MA-0065).
- `appeal.judicial.certiorari_review` — Certiorari review in superior court under c. 249 s.4 of a Supervisor order or untimely determination - MA&#x27;s designated judicial-review route (MA-0066).
- `appeal.judicial.civil_enforcement_action` — Direct de novo civil enforcement action in superior court, available without exhausting the Supervisor appeal (MA-0067, MA-0068).
- `enforcement.ag_referral_trigger` — Supervisor referral to the Attorney General on non-compliance with a Supervisor order (MA-0069).
- `custody.records_access_officer_designation` — Designated records access officer (municipal clerk by default) as the routing/response locus for all municipal requests (MA-0010, MA-0011, MA-0012).
