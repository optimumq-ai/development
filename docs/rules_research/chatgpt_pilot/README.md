*different* prompt/method than our discovery workflow. Preserved here so it persists (originals were
uploaded via WinSCP to the ephemeral exchange folder). **Read-only reference — not authoritative.**

  (S-001…S-012) + a 3-way config-source taxonomy (Statutory / Jurisdiction-policy / Operational).

**Coverage vs our discovery set (see `../wave*/`):** 32 state docs, **31 overlap** our 32. This set adds
**Kentucky** (we have not processed KY); it lacks **Nebraska** (we have NE).

**How it relates to our data (assessed 2026-07-23):**
- Complementary, not duplicate: these are workflow/state-machine + engine-design oriented; ours is atomic,
  verbatim, cited rules. Different lens.
- **Corroborates** our data where checked — NJ (7/14/21 business-day tracks) and MA (10/25/20/30 bd)
  matched our verbatim-cited rules exactly, same 2024 statutory amendments; CA/TX/TN/WA spot-checks agreed.
- **Cautions:** (1) broader scope than our target — they INCLUDE external appeal/review deadlines we are
  CUTTING in the relevance pass; don't let them re-introduce out-of-scope machinery. (2) Our verbatim+cited
  data stays the anchor; a claim here that isn't in our data is a lead to verify, not a fact to adopt.
  (3) Kentucky has not been through our verbatim/citation discipline. (4) It's a second AI pass, not law.

Intended uses: a completeness cross-check against our KEPT rules, and design-phase input (the config
