### Material negatives

- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No statutory duty to acknowledge a request within a fixed period. Arizona sets no numeric acknowledgment deadline; the only timing standard is &#x27;promptly furnish&#x27; (AZ-0011) and constructive denial on failure to promptly respond (AZ-0012). Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `deadlines.numeric_response_deadline` — No numeric statutory response/production deadline (no &#x27;X business days&#x27;). The standard is the soft &#x27;promptly&#x27; term. Captured as a soft-standard rule, not a value.
- `clarification.duty_to_assist_or_narrow` — No explicit statutory clarification clock, duty to assist the requester in narrowing an unclear or overbroad request, or non-response/abandonment trigger found in Title 39, Chapter 1.
- `request.tracking_log_requirement` — No statutory requirement that a municipality assign a tracking number or maintain a request log/register for public-records requests found.
- `redaction.segregability_duty` — No general statutory segregate-and-release / redaction-of-exempt-portions procedure stated in the core access statutes; withholding is instead documented via the § 39-121.01(D)(2) index (AZ-0015). Segregability obligations, if any, arise from specific exemption statutes and case law not inventoried this pass.
- `appeal.administrative_internal_stage` — No internal/administrative appeal stage for a denied requester before court; review is directly to superior court by special action (AZ-0025). Emitted affirmatively as the absence of an administrative appeal branch, relevant to cross-state comparison of appeal paths.
- `payment.deposit_for_large_requests` — No general statutory advance-deposit/estimate regime for large non-commercial requests; the only advance-payment authority found is for copying + postage on mailed copies (AZ-0022).
- `fee.statewide_copy_rate` — No statewide statutory per-page copy rate for ordinary (non-commercial) requests; the statute grants only permission to charge a fee (AZ-0018), leaving the value to the municipality.

### Material negatives

- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — CORA sets a response/inspection window (presumptive 3 working days, 24-72-203(3)(b)) but prescribes no separate mandatory acknowledgment-of-receipt deadline distinct from providing access. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `denial.deemed_denial_clock` — No express statutory &#x27;deemed denial&#x27; or constructive-denial clock (silence for N days = denial). CORA&#x27;s remedy trigger is an affirmative denial of access (24-72-204(4)-(5)); it does not convert non-response into an automatic denial after a fixed period. Keeper-sliver #3 therefore yields a negative for Colorado.
- `intake.written_request_required` — No statute mandates that a request be in writing or on a specific form. The custodian may adopt reasonable rules (24-72-203(2)(a)); intake format is thus a municipal-level rule, not a statewide fixed requirement.
- `payment.deposit_or_prepayment_cap` — The statute authorizes copy and research/retrieval fees but states no statutory deposit/prepayment cap or mandatory prepayment procedure. Custodian prepayment practices are local and not fixed by statute in the sections reviewed.
- `clarification.vague_request_clock` — No separate statutory clarification-and-toll mechanism for vague requests. A broadly stated/insufficiently specific request instead qualifies as an &#x27;extenuating circumstance&#x27; extending the response period (24-72-203(3)(b)); there is no distinct clarification clock that tolls or resets the deadline.
- `fee.commercial_purpose_surcharge` — The sections reviewed do not establish a general commercial-purpose fee surcharge for ordinary records. (Special provisions for records of names/addresses used for solicitation exist under 24-72-204/205.5 and belong to a later special-records pass.)
