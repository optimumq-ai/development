Actual Cost / Data Practices Office

Actual Cost
There are certain things that can be included when government calculates actual cost for the purpose of
charging for making copies of data to respond to a data request.

Things That Can Be Included
Cost of media (paper, USB flash drives, etc.)
Mailing costs
Employee time to prepare copies
Costs of reproduction that cannot be done by the entity, such as photographs (Advisory Opinions 95-044
(http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267781) and 97-012
(http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267345))
Employee time to search for and retrieve data for copying (Advisory Opinion 00-054
(http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-266998)) (Entities
cannot charge for search and retrieval time when the requestor is the data subject)

Things That Cannot Be Included
Employee time to separate public from not public data (Advisory Opinion 04-072
(http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267629))
Operating expenses of copier, such as electricity, wear and tear, etc. (Advisory Opinions 01-066
(http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267598) and 04-040
(http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267403))
Costs not related to copying, such as preparing an invoice (Advisory Opinion 04-055
(http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267937))
Returning data to off-site storage (Advisory Opinion 95-044 (http://mn.gov/admin/datapractices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267781))
Sorting, reviewing, or verifying accuracy if not necessary for copying (Advisory Opinion 04-072
(http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267629))
Sales tax (Advisory Opinions 94-059 (http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinionslibrary.jsp?id=36-267741) and 99-024 (http://mn.gov/admin/data-practices/opinions/library/opinionslibrary.jsp?id=36-267560))
Accounting functions (Advisory Opinion 04-003 (http://mn.gov/admin/datapractices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267827))
Costs related to inspection (Advisory Opinion 04-038 (http://mn.gov/admin/datapractices/opinions/library/opinions-library.jsp?id=36-267597))
