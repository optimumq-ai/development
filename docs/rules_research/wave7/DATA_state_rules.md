### Material negatives

- `meta.research_provenance` — Research date 2026-07-22. Michigan Freedom of Information Act, Act 442 of 1976, MCL 15.231-15.246. Session/law reviewed: codified MCL as published by the Michigan Legislature (Legislative Service Bureau), current through amendments in effect 2026; most recent operative amendments to the processing provisions are 2020 PA 36/38 (15.234, 15.235), 2018 PA 523 (15.233, 15.234). Codified statutes WERE available and opened directly (legislature.mi.gov); all source_language is verbatim from the opened statute. No 2021-2026 amendments to the core processing sections were found. Note: the Michigan FOIA statute&#x27;s TLS certificate failed in-tool WebFetch; text was retrieved via direct HTTPS (curl) against the official legislature.mi.gov Laws/MCL pages and confirmed against the on-page History lines.
- `intake.oral_request_permitted` — NEGATIVE for the &#x27;oral request permitted&#x27; concept: Michigan does NOT confer enforceable FOIA rights on oral requests. § 15.235(1) requires a written request; a body MAY informally provide records without a written request (§ 15.234(6), captured as MI-0068), but only a written request triggers the response/denial/appeal machinery. Emitted affirmatively as MI-0005 (written required).
- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No separate mere-acknowledgment deadline exists. Michigan&#x27;s 5-business-day window (MI-0016) requires a substantive disposition (grant/deny/partial/extend), not a bare acknowledgment of receipt.
- `clarification.nonresponse_clock` — No statutory clarification-and-toll mechanism. § 15.233(1) requires a request that &#x27;describes a public record sufficiently to enable the public body to find&#x27; it (MI-0006), but the act prescribes no formal clarification request, no clock that tolls pending clarification, and no cure/abandonment procedure for insufficiently described requests. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `deadline.production_after_grant` — No fixed statutory production/delivery deadline after a grant. The 5-business-day clock governs the response (grant/deny/extend); once granted, the act supplies only a nonbinding good-faith time-frame estimate accompanying any deposit (MI-0055), not a mandatory production date.
- `response.expedited_processing_track` — No general expedited-processing track for urgent public-interest requests. The only accelerated-handling provisions are the LE records-management-system referral (MI-0014) and the generic judicial &#x27;earliest practicable date&#x27; expediting of FOIA litigation (MI-0077).
- `fee.statewide_flat_rate` — No statewide flat search or copy fee. Fees are actual-incremental-cost with component ceilings (labor at lowest-paid-capable-employee wage; paper copies capped at $0.10/sheet). The city/public body sets operative values under those state-imposed ceilings.
- `appeal.administrative.mandatory` — The administrative appeal to the head of the public body (MI-0069) is optional, not a mandatory exhaustion prerequisite: § 15.240(1) lets the requester elect internal appeal OR a direct circuit-court action.

### Structural branches

- `eligibility.incarcerated_person_excluded` — Requester-class gate: individuals serving a sentence of imprisonment are not &#x27;persons&#x27; and cannot invoke FOIA (MI-0002).
- `routing.les_records_management_system` — Law-enforcement records-management-system host is a non-custodian; must issue a 10-business-day written referral directing the requester to the subscribing body (MI-0014).
- `custody.custodian_designation` — Mandatory FOIA-coordinator designation with statutory defaults (chief administrative officer / county board chair) and delegation authority (MI-0010, MI-0011, MI-0012).
- `denials.constructive_denial_trigger` — Silence beyond the response deadline becomes a final denial only if willful/intentional or the request carried FOIA-flagging language (MI-0022).
- `appeal.administrative.right` — Optional internal appeal to the head of the public body (10-business-day decision), a distinct stage from judicial review (MI-0069, MI-0070).
- `appeal.administrative.board_receipt_deeming` — When the head is a board/commission, the appeal-decision clock does not start until its next regularly scheduled meeting (MI-0072).
- `appeal.administrative.fee_appeal` — Separate excessive-fee appeal track (§ 15.240a) with its own 10-business-day administrative stage and 45-day judicial window (MI-0078, MI-0079, MI-0080).
- `appeal.judicial.fee_action_suspends_processing` — A filed fee-reduction civil action suspends the body&#x27;s duty to complete processing until the fee dispute is resolved (MI-0081).
- `payment.deposit_abandonment_trigger` — Deposit unpaid within 45 days of deemed receipt (with a 3-day mailing deem and 48-day due date) moves the request to an abandoned terminal state (MI-0059, MI-0060).
- `payment.prior_nonpayer_deposit` — Up-to-100% deposit branch for repeat requesters who left a prior request unpaid, gated by six conditions and terminated at 365 days (MI-0056, MI-0057).
- `fee.search_fee_unreasonable_cost_gate` — Search/examination/review/redaction labor may be charged only after the body finds and identifies that not charging would cause unreasonably high costs (MI-0045).
- `fee.no_fee_without_guidelines` — Bodies lacking published procedures/guidelines/public summary are barred from charging fees or requiring deposits until compliant (MI-0050).
- `coverage.judiciary_excluded` — The judiciary (and county clerk acting as circuit-court clerk) is outside &#x27;public body&#x27; and routes out of the FOIA workflow (MI-0087).
- `production.website_redirect` — Records available on the body&#x27;s website carry a no-labor-charge exemption plus a duty to redirect the requester to the specific webpage (MI-0046, MI-0047).

### Material negatives

- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No separate acknowledgment deadline distinct from the substantive response. The five-business-day § 901 window is the only initial deadline; silence for five business days is a deemed denial, not a missed acknowledgment.
- `intake.no_mandatory_form` — The statute does not mandate a particular form; § 703 allows any written request by several channels, and the OOR provides an optional standard form. No explicit statutory prohibition on requiring a form was located — treat &#x27;form optional&#x27; as guidance-level, verify.
- `deadline.receipt_day_not_counted` — OOR guidance/case law states the day the request is received is not counted and the five-business-day period starts the next business day. This computation rule was not confirmed in the codified statute text this pass — flag for OOR-regulation / case-law verification.
- `clarification.clarification_clock` — No express statutory clarification-and-tolling clock. A vague request is handled via the § 902(a)(7) extension ground and the specificity standard in § 703, not a dedicated clarification pause with its own deadline.
- `fee.commercial_purpose_surcharge` — The RTKL does not impose a distinct commercial-purpose fee surcharge on ordinary duplication; fees are governed by the OOR schedule and actual-cost limits regardless of purpose. (Contrast states with explicit commercial-use fee tiers.)
- `fee.search_time_charge` — No search/retrieval labor fee is authorized for ordinary requests; § 1307(g) bars charging for access-determination review, and duplication fees are the primary permitted charge. Staff-time charges appear only in narrow contexts — verify against OOR guidance.
- `preservation.litigation_hold_on_request` — No explicit statutory record-preservation/litigation-hold triggered by the filing of a request or appeal was located in the RTKL text this pass; general retention is governed by separate records-management law (out of scope).

### Structural branches

- `appeal.administrative.appeals_officer_duties` — PA-specific administrative appeal stage before the Office of Open Records (Commonwealth/local agencies) or a designated judicial/legislative/other appeals officer — a mandatory review path with its own scheduling, document exchange, discretionary hearing, and 30-day final determination (§§ 1101-1102).
- `appeal.judicial.common_pleas_window` — Two-track judicial review fork the engine must express: local-agency decisions go to the court of common pleas of the county (§ 1302), while Commonwealth/legislative/judicial-agency decisions go to Commonwealth Court (§ 1301).
- `deadline.extension_grounds` — Extension branch: on any of seven enumerated § 902(a) factors the request moves into a review state requiring a written extension notice within 5 business days, an expected-response date, and a 30-day cap gated on written requester agreement.
- `production.trade_secret_notice_window` — Third-party trade-secret/confidential-proprietary sub-process (§ 707(b)): a chained notice-and-input workflow (5 business days to notify, 5 for third-party input, 10 to decide) that the request enters when a flagged third-party record is implicated.
- `redaction.redaction_is_denial` — Any redaction is legally reclassified as a partial denial under Chapter 9 (§ 706), so a redacted production must branch into the denial/appeal machinery, not close as a grant.
- `classification.discretionary_release_exempt` — Agency-head discretionary-release path (§ 506(c)): a balancing branch allowing release of otherwise-exempt records when public interest outweighs restriction — requires human legal judgment, not auto-configuration.
- `production.electronic_means_response` — Electronic-availability response fork (§ 704): agency may close a request by pointing to publicly accessible electronic means, which opens a requester right to demand paper conversion (30-day request / 5-day agency turnaround).
