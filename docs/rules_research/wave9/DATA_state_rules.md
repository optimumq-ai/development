### Material negatives

- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — Idaho sets NO statutory per-page cents copy rate. Copy fees are bound only to &#x27;actual cost&#x27; (74-102(10)(c)); the city supplies the operative rate under that ceiling.
- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No separate acknowledgment-of-receipt requirement found; the statute requires the agency to &#x27;grant or deny&#x27; within the response window (74-103), not a distinct acknowledgment.
- `clarification.request_permitted` — No explicit clarification clock or nonresponse-abandonment provision located in Title 74 Ch. 1. The &#x27;clearly indicate it is a public records request&#x27; requirement (ID-0005) is the closest intake gate; absence of a clarification rule is not proof none exists.
- `enforcement.burden_of_proof` — The retrieved 74-115 text did not include an explicit burden-of-proof-on-agency, in-camera-examination, or de-novo-review clause. Requires manual verification of the full 74-115 text before asserting or excluding these.
- `custody.foia_officer_designation` — Idaho does not appear to require formally designating/publishing a public-records officer; it references the existing &#x27;custodian&#x27; (74-101(3)). Contrast IL/WA which mandate an officer designation.
- `special.access_to_own_records` — Idaho Code 74-113 (Access to Records About a Person by a Person) creates a distinct access-to-own-records workflow, and 74-120 prohibits use of records for mailing/telephone lists (driving the permitted custodian inquiry). Not fully captured this pass; flag for a special-records / distinct-workflow follow-up.
- `deadline.time_unit_ambiguity` — Resident windows are stated in &#x27;working days&#x27; (3 / 10) while nonresident windows are stated in plain &#x27;days&#x27; (21 / 35). Whether nonresident days are calendar days (assumed here) should be confirmed against the exact 74-103(2) text.

### Structural branches

- `residency.two_track_processing` — The 2025 amendment (ch. 298) creates a resident vs. nonresident fork determined by a sworn residency declaration (ID-0007): residents get a 3-working-day response window and free allotments (2 labor hours / 100 pages); nonresidents get a 21-day window and no free allotments. The engine must branch every request on residency status.
- `production.format_conversion_extension` — Distinct electronic-format conversion sub-path (ID-0012): when a record must be converted and cannot be delivered in the allotted time, the agency issues a written notice and delivers at a mutually agreed time -- a bespoke soft-deadline stage separate from the ordinary locate/retrieve extension.
- `denial.deemed_denial_terminal_state` — Nonresponse within the allotted window is a terminal deemed-denial state (ID-0013) that ends normal processing and opens the judicial appeal path.
- `appeal.judicial.only_path` — Idaho&#x27;s Act provides only a judicial appeal path -- petition to district court (ID-0027) within 180 days (ID-0028). There is no internal/administrative appeal stage to model.

### Material negatives

- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — GRAMA sets no separate acknowledgment/receipt-confirmation deadline distinct from the substantive 10/5-business-day response window in § 63G-2-204(4). No found rule requiring an interim acknowledgment.
- `clarification.clock_or_abandonment` — No explicit clarification clock or nonresponse-abandonment/withdrawal procedure found. A request must be reasonably specific (§ 63G-2-204(1)(a)(ii)) but GRAMA does not codify a tolling clarification exchange or deem an unclarified request abandoned. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `deadlines.day_computation_rule` — Chapter 2 contains no codified Saturday/Sunday/holiday day-counting rule. Guidance (Utah State Archives / ombudsman) states business days exclude weekends and legal holidays and that periods over 11 days are counted as calendar days, but this was not located in the statute itself this pass — flagged for an administrative-rule / guidance pass.
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide per-page copy rate. Fees are actual-cost-based (§ 63G-2-203) and set by each political subdivision by ordinance/policy (§ 63G-2-203(3)(c)); the per-page value is a city-supplied parameter, not a state number.
- `payment.deposit_percentage` — No fixed deposit percentage; prepayment is of past plus estimated future fees in full, gated by the $50 threshold (§ 63G-2-203(8)). No partial-deposit formula found.
- `eligibility.no_purpose_requirement` — Related caveat: GRAMA has no explicit anti-purpose-discrimination clause in current § 63G-2-201; the no-purpose fact (UT-0003) is inferred from the request-content list. An older/handbook statement that access cannot be restricted based on the reason for the request should be verified against statute/case law in a later pass.

- `response.extraordinary_circumstances_notice` — Request enters an extended-processing state on an extraordinary-circumstances claim (UT-0014/0015), with ground-specific extension deadlines (UT-0016 5-day legal review; UT-0017 15-day editing; UT-0018 soft ASAP for volume/programming).

- `appeal.administrative.local_appeals_board` — Municipal fork (UT-0057): a political subdivision may create a 3-member local appeals board; if it does, the second-stage appeal of a CAO decision goes to that board rather than to the director of the Government Records Office.
- `appeal.administrative.director_review` — State-level second-stage appeal to the director of the Government Records Office (UT-0056/0059/0060), a distinct adjudicative body with its own hearing scheduling (16-64 day) and a 73-calendar-day deemed-denial backstop.
- `appeal.mediation_tolls_appeal_clock` — Ombudsman-mediation branch (UT-0058): submitting a § 63A-12-204 mediation request tolls both the director-appeal and judicial-review filing windows until the ombudsman certifies conclusion or non-occurrence.
- `intake.vexatious_requester_petition` — Vexatious-requester adjudication before the director (UT-0051/0052/0053): an entity can petition to be relieved (up to one year) of the duty to fulfill a person&#x27;s requests; such blocked requests are not appealable under Part 4.
- `redaction.business_confidentiality_claim` — Business-confidentiality workflow (UT-0062/0063/0064): a submitter&#x27;s trade-secret claim triggers claimant notice on appeal (3-day / 7-day) and a disclosure hold pending appeal.
- `response.political_subdivision_alt_timeframes` — Municipal timeframe fork (UT-0049): a city&#x27;s ordinance may set response and appeal deadlines different from the statutory defaults where resources are insufficient — the operative per-request deadlines can be city-specific.
- `denial.constructive_denial_on_silence` — Deemed-denial state transition (UT-0019): silence past the response window becomes an appealable denial. Parallel deemed outcomes exist at the CAO stage (UT-0042) and director stage (UT-0060).

### Material negatives

- `denial.deemed_denial_on_nonresponse` — No express statute deeming custodian silence a denial. Nebraska instead imposes an affirmative four-business-day duty (§ 84-712(4)) plus remedies for &#x27;denial&#x27; (§ 84-712.03); a failure to respond is treated as reviewable noncompliance rather than a statutorily &#x27;deemed denial.&#x27; Absence of a found deemed-denial trigger is not proof none exists.
- `redaction.segregability` — No standalone &#x27;reasonably segregable — release the rest&#x27; production mandate located as a discrete clause. Partial coverage exists: § 84-712.04(1) requires denials to correlate specific withheld portions to specific reasons, and § 84-712(1) references &#x27;physically redacting,&#x27; both implying portion-level redaction. Verify whether a freestanding segregability duty exists.
- `custody.records_officer_designation` — No statutory requirement that a public body designate/publicly identify a records or FOIA officer. Duties run to the &#x27;lawful custodian&#x27; (whoever holds custody); no designation-and-publication step was found.
- `preservation.hold_pending_request` — No express provision requiring retention/preservation of a requested record while the request or an appeal is pending. Routine retention schedules are governed separately and excluded.
- `fee.per_page_rate_ceiling` — No statewide fixed per-page copy rate. Copy fees are capped only at &#x27;actual added cost&#x27; (§ 84-712(1), NE-0019); the municipality supplies the operative rate under that ceiling.
- `clarification.request_permitted` — No separate custodian-initiated clarification clock. The nearest mechanism is the post-estimate 10-business-day requester window to negotiate/narrow (§ 84-712(4), NE-0015), which is requester-driven rather than a custodian clarification demand.
- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No acknowledgment step distinct from the substantive four-business-day response; the single four-day duty (NE-0011) subsumes acknowledgment.
- `EXCLUDED.criminal_penalty` — EXCLUDED (inclusion test / Remove list — criminal penalties): § 84-712.09 makes a violating official subject to removal or impeachment and guilty of a Class III misdemeanor. Nothing in per-request software processing changes; recorded as audit trail, not a rule.
- `EXCLUDED.attorney_fees` — EXCLUDED (Remove list — attorney-fee provisions): § 84-712.07 authorizes equitable relief and reasonable attorney fees/litigation costs to a substantially prevailing complainant. Remedy amount, not a request-processing step.
- `EXCLUDED.exemption_catalog` — EXCLUDED (exemptions not inventoried): § 84-712.05 lists ~30 discretionary categories a lawful custodian &#x27;may withhold.&#x27; Only the general discretionary-withholding posture is captured via classification.presumption_of_openness (NE-0004) and the denial-specificity rules; the individual confidentiality categories do not each create a distinct processing workflow and are not enumerated as rows.

### Structural branches

- `response.options_enumerated` — The initial response forks into three engine-executable paths: grant access/copies, written denial, or written explanation of delay (Neb. Rev. Stat. § 84-712(4)) — NE-0013.
- `communication.delay_certification_date_certain` — Option (c) delay path: written explanation with earliest-practicable-date, cost estimate, and requester modify/prioritize opportunity when the request is significantly difficult or extensive — NE-0014.
- `clarification.cost_review_window` — Post-estimate requester-action stage (10 business days) to fulfill, negotiate, or withdraw, added by 2024 LB 43 — NE-0015; nonresponse halts fulfillment (NE-0016).
- `appeal.admin.review_right` — Attorney General review path as an alternative to court: petition → AG determination in 15 calendar days → possible AG-brought enforcement suit — NE-0033/0034/0035.
- `appeal.judicial.enforcement_right` — Mandamus enforcement path in district court with docket precedence and burden on the public body — NE-0032/0031/0036.
- `denial.denial_log_maintained` — Denial-letter file each public body must maintain and expose to the public on request — NE-0030.

### Material negatives

- `intake.acknowledgment_window` — No separate receipt-acknowledgment requirement distinct from the substantive determination. SC&#x27;s first statutory duty is the 10-business-day (20 for records &gt;24 months) determination-and-reasons notice under § 30-4-30(C); there is no earlier &#x27;we received your request&#x27; acknowledgment obligation. Absence of a found rule is not proof none exists.
- `fee.copy_rate_per_page` — No statewide fixed per-page copy rate. § 30-4-30(B) caps copy fees at the &#x27;prevailing commercial rate&#x27; (a ceiling) and requires uniformity, but sets no cents-per-page number; the city supplies the value under the commercial-rate ceiling (see SC-0009).
- `clarification.duty_to_assist_or_clarify` — No statutory duty for the public body to help a requester clarify or narrow a request, and no clarification clock that tolls or restarts the response deadlines. The body&#x27;s remedy for a vague/overbroad request is to seek a court hearing under § 30-4-110(A) (SC-0038), not a statutory clarify-and-toll mechanism.
- `appeal.administrative.internal_review` — No statutory administrative or internal-agency appeal stage, and no AG/records-authority adjudicative review of denials. Enforcement is exclusively judicial via circuit court (§ 30-4-100). The SC Attorney General issues advisory FOIA opinions but has no statutory power to compel disclosure.
- `intake.submission_method_required` — No statutorily prescribed request form, portal, or delivery channel. § 30-4-30(C) requires a &#x27;written request&#x27; to trigger the response duties but does not mandate a particular medium or form (email, letter, and portal all qualify if written).
- `deadlines.determination_extension_by_body` — No unilateral good-cause extension of the § 30-4-30(C) determination/production clocks by the public body. Extensions require written MUTUAL agreement of the body and requester (SC-0027); the statute provides no body-only extension for volume/complexity.
- `production.installment_rolling_release` — No statutory rolling/installment production scheme (records produced in tranches with staggered deadlines). The statute sets a single production deadline (30/35 calendar days) from determination or deposit receipt.

### Structural branches

- `eligibility.incarcerated_person_exclusion` — Requester-class gate: incarcerated persons are excluded from the FOIA inspect/copy right (constitutional discovery rights preserved). § 30-4-30(A)(1).
- `production.walk_in_inspection_no_written_request` — Alternate intake/production path: enumerated record classes (recent minutes, incident reports, confinement docs, meeting-distributed docs, within stated lookback windows) available in person, immediately, without a written request or determination clock. § 30-4-30(D).
- `production.website_deemed_compliance` — Compliance-by-publication branch: posting the (D) records online satisfies the in-person availability duty. § 30-4-30(E).
- `denials.deemed_granted_on_nonresponse` — Terminal state: silence past the 10/20-business-day determination deadline = request deemed approved as to nonexempt records (exemptions not waived). § 30-4-30(C).
- `fee.waiver_public_interest` — Discretionary fee decision path: waive/reduce/charge based on public-interest / primary-benefit-to-general-public finding. § 30-4-30(B).
- `fee.legislator_no_charge` — Special-requester branch: General Assembly members pay no charge for legislative-duty requests. § 30-4-30(B).
- `appeal.judicial.declaratory_injunctive` — SC&#x27;s only enforcement path is judicial (circuit court declaratory/injunctive), citizen-of-the-State standing, 1-year limit, with statutory 10-day-to-hearing and 6-month-to-conclude court clocks. No administrative/internal appeal exists. § 30-4-100.
- `review.public_body_hearing_burdensome` — Body-initiated diversion path: the public body routes an improper/burdensome request, or one it cannot resolve for exemption, to circuit court instead of answering. § 30-4-110(A).
- `review.third_party_reverse_foia_hearing` — Reverse-FOIA branch: a third party with a specific interest may demand a hearing/intervene before release of records in enumerated § 30-4-40(a) exempt categories. § 30-4-110(B).
- `special.bodycam_agency_hearing_deadline` — Special-record workflow for law-enforcement vehicle/dashboard recordings of qualifying officer-involved incidents: 15-business-day agency hearing window, notice to requester, in-camera hearing, clear-and-convincing standard. § 30-4-50(A)(9).
