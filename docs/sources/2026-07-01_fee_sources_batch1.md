## Birmingham, Alabama
- **State** — Ala. Code §§36-12-40, 36-12-41 + 2024 amendments. https://law.justia.com/codes/alabama/title-36/chapter-12/article-3/section-36-12-41/ ; ALM revised 2025-04-30: https://almonline.org/Assets/Files/LegalSelectedReadings/15.Public-Records-REVISED-04-30-2025.pdf
  - Copy on proper request + payment of a reasonable fee. No uniform municipal schedule; fees framed as "reasonable" unless another statute sets an amount.
- **City** — Birmingham Public Records Request Form + Duplication/Access Fee Schedule. https://www.birminghamal.gov/government/city-departments/city-clerks-office/publicrecords-request ; form PDF: https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/assets.birminghamal.gov/s3fspublic/2024-12/Public-Records-Request-Form-2024.pdf
  - Paper $0.50/1-side, $0.75/2-side, B&W only (no color). Electronic $8.00/media unit (CD/flash/email) + $0.10/page. Certified +$5.00/copy. No charge for routine inspection (nominal clerical). **"Pre-payment is not accepted... records will not be released until full payment is received."** (pay-in-full-before-release delivery trigger.)

## Fayetteville, Arkansas
- **State** — Ark. Code Ann. §25-19-105(d)(3). https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-25/chapter-19/section-25-19-105/ ; AG FOIA: https://arkansasag.gov/divisions/opinions-foia/arkansas-freedom-of-information-act/ ; 2025 FOIA Handbook 21st ed: https://healthy.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025-FOIA-Handbook-21st-Edition-ver4.pdf
  - Copies ≤ actual reproduction cost (medium/supplies/equipment/maintenance), **excluding personnel search/retrieval/review/copy time** except as provided. Mailing/transmission actual cost. **Advance payment if estimate > $25.** Itemized breakdown required. Waiver/reduction for noncommercial public-interest. Exempt/nonexempt separation cost borne by custodian.
- **City** — FOIA/police pages; **no citywide fee/deposit schedule located.** https://www.fayetteville-ar.gov/3425/Freedom-of-Information-Act-Request ; https://www.fayetteville-ar.gov/551/Openness-in-City-Government-andFOIA ; https://www.fayetteville-ar.gov/243/Records-Division
  - Online FOIA submission for reports/dash/body cam/911. Accident reports 5–7 business days; state-required $10 accident-report fee (in person).

## Tulsa, Oklahoma
- **State** — 51 O.S. §24A.5, am. SB535 eff. 2025-11-01. https://oklegal.onenet.net/oklegal-cgi/get_statute?97%2FTitle.51%2F51-24A.5.html= ; SB535 enrolled: https://legiscan.com/OK/text/SB535/id/3281930/Oklahoma-2026-SB535-Enrolled.pdf
  - Copies ≤ $0.25/page (≤ 8.5×14), certified ≤ $1.00/page, unless other law. Search fee only if **solely commercial** OR **clearly excessive disruption of essential functions**. No search fee where public interest (news media/scholars/authors/taxpayers evaluating gov). **Advance payment if estimate > $75** or prior outstanding fees; overpayments returned. Must post written schedule at principal office + county clerk. Fees not to discourage requests.
- **City** — EO No. 2024-08; Tulsa PD page. https://www.cityoftulsa.org/media/26553/executive-order-no-2024-08.pdf ; https://www.tulsapolice.org/openrecordsact
  - EO adopts City ORA fee schedule; no charge for records prepared for public distribution; schedule posted online + City Clerk. Custodians collect + deposit to City registry. Tulsa PD (City Ord. 19224): $3.00 / ≤10 pages, then $1.00/page over ten.

## Charlotte, North Carolina
- **State** — N.C. Gen. Stat. §132-6.2. https://www.ncleg.net/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_132/gs_132-6.2.html ; Ch. 132 index: https://www.ncleg.gov/Laws/GeneralStatuteSections/Chapter132
  - Copies in any available medium; fees ≤ actual reproduction cost. Reasonable **special service charge** (actual/labor cost) if extensive IT or clerical/supervisory assistance. **Fee-dispute mediation via State CIO/designee.** Computer-database copies may require written request.
- **City** — Public Records Request page; **no citywide fee/deposit schedule located.** https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Request-a-Public-Record
  - Portal-directed; records open unless exempt. NOTE: a UNC Charlotte policy (4-hr threshold, $18/hr, 50% deposit >$500) surfaces in search but is NOT City policy: https://legal.charlotte.edu/policies/up-605-8/

## Atlanta, Georgia
- **State** — O.C.G.A. §50-18-71(c)-(d). https://law.justia.com/codes/georgia/title-50/chapter-18/article-4/section-50-18-71/ ; SOS: https://sos.ga.gov/page/georgia-open-records
  - Search/retrieval/redaction at hourly salary of lowest-paid FTE with the skill; **first 15 min free**. Copies ≤ $0.10/page (letter/legal); other at actual; electronic + media actual cost. **If charges > $25: notify within ≤ 3 business days with estimate**; may defer until requester agrees to pay. **If estimated production > $500: may insist on prepayment** before search/retrieval/review/production. Prior unpaid → prepay future until resolved.
- **City** — Office of Transparency; APD open records. https://www.atlantaga.gov/government/mayor-s-office/executive-offices/office-of-transparency ; https://www.atlantapd.org/i-want-to/open-records-request-new
  - Mirrors state: 3-business-day response; estimated timeframe if longer; lowest-paid FTE rate, charges begin after first 15 min; prepay if est > $500 or prior unpaid. APD: research fee ≤ lowest-paid FTE hourly; $0.10/page; $1.00/disc or tape.

## Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania
- **State** — RTKL 65 P.S. §67.1307 + OOR official fee schedule. https://www.openrecords.pa.gov/rtkl/about.cfm ; schedule (updated 2022-12-30): https://www.openrecords.pa.gov/Documents/RTKL/Official_RTKL_Fee_Schedule.pdf
  - OOR schedule governs Commonwealth + local agencies; no other fees unless authorized/necessarily-incurred reasonable costs. **No fee for access-determination review.** B&W ≤ $0.25 first 1,000 copies, ≤ $0.20 beyond (volume step-down); color ≤ $0.50; specialized ≤ actual; email no fee; CD/DVD ≤ $1/disc; postage actual; certification ≤ $5/record; **redaction no additional fee**.
- **City** — Right to Know pages. https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/City-Government/Legal-Services/Department-of-Law/Right-to-Know ; .../Making-a-Request
  - Records under PA RTKL; medium as requested if it exists; not required to create/compile/format a nonexistent record. No separate municipal schedule exposed; state OOR schedule controls unless another statute applies.

## Dearborn, Michigan
- **State** — MCL 15.234 (FOIA Act 442 of 1976). https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=MCL-15-234 ; PDF: https://www.legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/pdf/mcl-act-442-of-1976.pdf  *(VERIFIED — see map §E)*
  - Labor (search/locate/examine; separation/deletion; duplication/publication) at ≤ lowest-paid capable; nonpaper media actual; paper ≤ $0.10/sheet (8.5×11 / 8.5×14); economical/double-sided if cheaper. **Fringe add-on ≤ 50%** if itemized; overtime excluded unless requested. **Deposit ≤ 50% if estimate > $50.** **100% deposit** for subsequent requests with prior unpaid (statutory conditions).
- **City** — Dearborn FOIA Filing Procedures & Guidelines. https://dearborn.gov/sites/default/files/2024-06/Dearborn%20FOIA%20Filing%20Procedures%20and%20Guidelines.pdf ; NextRequest: https://cityofdearbornmi.nextrequest.com/requests/new
  - ≤ $50 → notified of amount due + pickup. > $50 (or prior unpaid) → good-faith deposit before processing; deposit ≤ 50% of estimate. 100% deposit for delinquent under conditions (prior final ≤ 105% of est; delivered in timeframe; 90 days post-notice; no proof of payment; itemization). Waived if proof of prior payment, subsequently paid, or 365 days elapsed. Labor for search/location/exam if not charging = unreasonably high cost; review/separation; media actual; economical mailing.

## Boise, Idaho
- **State** — Idaho Code §74-102(10). https://law.justia.com/codes/idaho/title-74/chapter-1/section-74-102/ ; Dept. of Finance schedule: https://www.finance.idaho.gov/legal/public-records-requests/publicrecords-request-fee-schedule/ ; AG: https://www.ag.idaho.gov/office-resources/public-records/
  - **First 2 labor hours + first 100 pages free** (unless otherwise authorized). Beyond → actual labor + copy cost if > 100 pages, redaction needed, or labor > 2 person-hours. Charges based on lowest-paid employee and/or attorney necessary. Advance payment allowed; overpayments returned.
- **City** — Boise Public Records Access / Research Request. https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/city-clerk/public-records/publicrecords-access-research-request/ ; police: https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/police/police-public-records-request/
  - Initial response within 3 business days. Most requests free; payment if > 100 printed pages or > 2 hrs staff time. Estimate provided; **payment of estimate required before work begins**; additional payment if actual exceeds estimate.
