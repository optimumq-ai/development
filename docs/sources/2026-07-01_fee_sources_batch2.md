## Sioux Falls, South Dakota
- **State** — SDCL ch. 1-27; §§1-27-1.2, 1-27-35/36/38. https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/1-27
  - §1-27-1.2 "Fees for specialized service." §1-27-35 informal requests / retrieval-reproduction costs; §1-27-36 estimate of retrieval+reproduction cost / waiver-reduction; **§1-27-38 civil action or administrative review of denial or fee estimate.** Fees tied to actual retrieval/reproduction; specialized-service charge separate; statutory mechanism to object to estimates.
- **City** — City Clerk records page; **no citywide fee/deposit schedule located.** https://www.siouxfalls.gov/government/council/city-clerk

## Des Moines, Iowa
- **State** — Iowa Code §22.3. https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/22.3.pdf
  - Requester pays all reasonable examination/copying expenses. Custodian may charge reasonable supervision fee. Copying fee ≤ **actual cost**. Actual costs = only reasonable expenses directly attributable to supervising exam + making/providing copies; **exclude ordinary/overhead — benefits, depreciation, maintenance, electricity, insurance.** Legal-services cost only for redaction/review of confidential info. IPIB guidance: communicate estimate on receipt; may require payment before retrieval.
- **City** — Des Moines PD Public Records Requests. https://www.dsm.city/departments/police/administrative/public_records.php
  - Application for Public Records; "fees vary per state law, calculated per event." Simple requests 3–5 business days; video/audio/photos longer. No broader citywide schedule located; defers to state + per-event.

## Albuquerque, New Mexico
- **State** — NMSA 1978 §14-2-9 (IPRA). https://law.justia.com/codes/new-mexico/chapter-14/article-2/section-14-2-9/
  - (A) separate exempt/nonexempt; (B) electronic format if available + requested (existing file format only); (C)(1) reasonable copy fees unless law prescribes; (C)(2) **≤ $1.00/page** (≤ 11×17); (C)(3)-(4) actual cost for disk/storage + mail/email/fax. DOJ guidance: **no fee for electronically-produced records**; print 8.5×11 = $0.75/page, larger $1/page, CD/DVD $10/disc; may request copying fees in advance.
- **City** — ABQ Records / Right to Inspect. https://www.cabq.gov/clerk/public-records
  - Portal / mail / in-person; right to inspect under IPRA §§14-2-1..12; links Notice of Right to Inspect + Fee Waiver Request. No separate citywide schedule located.

## Mesa, Arizona
- **State** — ARS §§39-121.01, 39-121.03. https://www.azleg.gov/ars/39/00121-01.htm  *(commercial market-value basis — pending verification, map §D/§E)*
  - §39-121.01(D)(1): may request copies/printouts/photos; may require advance payment for mailed copies; custodian promptly furnishes, may charge a fee if facilities available. **§39-121.03(A) commercial-purpose: charge may include (a) portion of cost of obtaining original, (b) reasonable time/materials/equipment/personnel fee, AND (c) VALUE OF THE REPRODUCTION ON THE COMMERCIAL MARKET.** §39-121.03(C): liability for obtaining commercial-purpose records without proper disclosure / misuse of noncommercial records.
- **City** — Mesa Public Records Requests. https://www.mesaaz.gov/Government/Public-Records-Requests
  - Many records pre-posted online (budget, permits, code, agendas/minutes, crime stats, contracts, zoning). No separate citywide fee/deposit schedule located; AZ statutes + dept schedules control.

## Henderson, Nevada
- **State** — NRS ch. 239 (§239.052 fees, §239.055 extraordinary-use); NAC §239.864. https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/nrs-239.html
  - §239.052 copy-fee limits/waiver/posting; §239.055 **extraordinary-use fees**. NAC §239.864 (Exec. Dept.): **estimate if actual cost > $25** (incl. postage if mailed); may require **deposit ≤ estimated actual cost**; **require payment (incl. postage) before requester receives the copy.**
- **City** — Henderson Public Records Policy + Records Fee Schedule. https://www.cityofhenderson.com/government/departments/city-clerk/public-records/public-records-policy
  - Policy: orderly procedure; fee schedule to reimburse actual costs; citizen/media guidance. Links "City-wide Public Records and Document Services Fee Table Oct 2025 Final." Fees per NRS 239.055; charges for copies + staff research time.

## Chesapeake, Virginia
- **State** — Va. Code §2.2-3704(F)-(I). https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title2.2/chapter37/section2.2-3704/  *(VERIFIED — map §E)*
  - (F) reasonable charges ≤ actual cost (access/duplicate/supply/search); lowest-possible-cost effort; **no extraneous/intermediary/surplus/general-business fees**; duplication ≤ actual. Before search, notify charges may apply + ask if requester wants an estimate; provide if requested. **Response tolled between estimate notice and requester response; deemed withdrawn if no response in 30 days.** (H) **deposit up to advance determination if charges likely > $200**, credited to final. (I) may require payment of amounts unpaid ≥ 30 days before processing.
- **City** — Chesapeake FOIA: Costs. https://www.cityofchesapeake.net/3210/Freedom-of-Information-Act
  - Reimburse actual costs incl. staff search time; no overhead. 5-day clock excludes estimate-approval interval; request closed if no response in 30 days. May require payment of prior FOIA bills > 30 days past due before new request.

## Providence, Rhode Island
- **State** — R.I. Gen. Laws §38-2-4. https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/statutes/title38/38-2/38-2-4.HTM
  - (a) copies ≤ **$0.15/page** (common business/legal); electronic + storage-retrieval ≤ reasonable actual. (b) search/retrieval ≤ **$15/hour**, **first hour free**; **multiple requests from same person/entity to same body within 30 days = one request.** (c) reasonable time; estimate before copies on request. (d) itemization on request. (e) court may reduce/waive for public interest (not primarily commercial).
- **City** — Providence APRA procedures. https://www.providenceri.gov/law-department/public-records-request/
  - $0.15/page; search/retrieval ≤ $15/hr; first hour free; estimate before fulfilling (chance to retract/amend); prepayment tolls response per §38-2-7(b); same-requester within 30 days = one request per §38-2-4(b).

## Eugene, Oregon
- **State** — ORS §§192.324, 192.329. https://law.justia.com/codes/oregon/volume-05/chapter-192/section-192-324/
  - §192.324(4)(a) fees reasonably calc'd to actual cost incl. summarizing/compiling/tailoring. (4)(b) **attorney time for review/redaction/segregation billable, but NOT time determining whether the records law applies.** (4)(c) fee > **$25** requires written estimate + requester confirmation to proceed. (5)-(6) waiver if primarily public benefit; petition review. (7) must publish written fee-calculation procedure.
- **City** — Eugene Public Records. https://www.eugene-or.gov/519/Public-Records
  - ≤ 30 min + no resources → free (Level 3). Fees by Administrative Order. **Costs communicated before fulfilling; paid before retrieval begins.** Notified if costs exceed estimate; refund if less. **60 days to pay or request closed** (ORS 192.329(3)(b)). Level 2/1 require written request; estimate; pay before retrieval.

## Springfield, Missouri
- **State** — RSMo §610.026. https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?bid=30423&section=610.026
  - .1(1) paper (≤ 9×14) ≤ **$0.10/page**; duplication time ≤ average clerical hourly; research time at actual cost; must staff to lowest total charge. Estimate on request. Waiver/reduction for public-interest (not primarily commercial). .1(2) computer/media: copies + staff time (≤ avg hourly) + programming if needed + medium cost; special expertise (maps/blueprints) + beyond-customary programming at actual.
- **City** — Springfield Sunshine Law. https://www.springfieldmo.gov/241/Sunshine-Law  (page returned 403 to browsing tool; online Public Records Center)
  - No separate citywide municipal schedule accessible. Local example (Springfield Public Schools): ≤ $0.10/page standard + reasonable search time; estimate by end of 3rd business day; documents provided once fee paid.
