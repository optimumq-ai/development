What Texas law says
Loaded when the state was locked. Not editable — click a citation to read the statute text behind it.
2 rules
TX-0005
A request must be in writing and delivered by U.S. mail, e-mail, hand delivery, or another method the city has approved — for example, fax or its website.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.234(a)
TX-0006
The city may designate one mailing address and one e-mail address for requests; if it posts them, it need not respond to a request that arrives anywhere else (hand delivery and approved methods excepted).
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.234(c), (d)
What this already means for the city
In writing, always  a phone call is never a valid Texas request — no phone channel exists to switch on.
Designated addresses  the mailing and contact e-mail addresses entered on the agency screen serve as the designated ones — shown to requestors on the portal and in every letter. Changing them is an agency-screen act, not a choice here.
