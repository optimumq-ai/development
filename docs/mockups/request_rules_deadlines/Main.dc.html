What Texas law says
Loaded when the state was locked. Not editable — click a citation to read the statute text behind it.
4 rules
TX-0008
Public information must be produced promptly — as soon as possible under the circumstances, within a reasonable time, without delay.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.221(a), (b)
TX-0009
If the information cannot be produced within 10 business days, the officer must certify that in writing and set a date and hour when it will be available.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.221(d)
TX-0010
Information in active use or in storage gets the same written certification, with a date and hour when it will be available.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.221(c)
TX-S04
A body significantly impacted by a catastrophe may suspend requirements for up to 7 days, once extendable by 7 — requests during the suspension count as received the first business day after it ends; pending clocks pause.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.233
