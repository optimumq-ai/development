Re: Public Records Request 2026-000012

Dear Jordan Sample,

Thank you for your public records request (2026-000012). Before we can continue, we need a little more information to be sure we locate the right records.

Here is what we have on file for your request:

"Any records about the road work on my street."

To help us proceed, please reply with any additional detail you can provide — for example, the specific records, names, dates, or time period you are interested in, and the department or topic they relate to. The more specific you can be, the faster we can respond.

Please reply within 61 days. If we do not hear from you, we may be unable to continue processing your request.

Please keep your request number (2026-000012) with any reply so we can match your response to the correct request.

Questions? Contact us at openrecords@cityofautumnfalls.gov or 369-867-5309.

Sincerely,
City of Autumn Falls

The highlighted sentence is the warning Texas requires (§ 552.222(e)) — the letter must say what happens if the requestor does not reply. The standard sentence says "we may be unable to continue"; the statute's actual consequence is that the request is treated as withdrawn on the 61st day. See the note beside this frame.
