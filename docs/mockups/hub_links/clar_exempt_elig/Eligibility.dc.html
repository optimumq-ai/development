What Texas law says

4 rules
TX-0001
Any person may request public information, and the city must promptly produce it for inspection, copies, or both.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.221(a)
TX-0002
The city may not ask what the information will be used for.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.222(b)
TX-0003
There is no residency requirement — "any person" means anyone, wherever they live.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.221(a)
TX-0004
The city is not required to accept a request from an incarcerated or confined person, or that person's agent — except the person's attorney asking for information that may be disclosed.
Tex. Gov't Code § 552.028(a)
