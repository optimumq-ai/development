Waiting until Where the records live is reviewed
This is the one option where the hub genuinely shows work in flight. It is also the one where a step can sit still for a week because one person is on leave — and note that it forces a sixth state onto the page, In review, which the other two do not need.
Why you might want this
It is the only option that actually guarantees a second pair of eyes before a later step builds on an earlier one. For a city rolling this out across several departments, it also gives the Director a single place to see what is being set up and by whom.
What it costs you
Setup now stalls on people. In a small city the reviewer and the person who did the work are often the same person, which turns the whole ceremony into clicking your own name twice. It also needs a queue, a notification, and somewhere for the reviewer to say no and explain why — the largest build of the three.
What it needs from the role model
A reviewer for each lane, and the authority to approve somebody else's work. That is the authority axis of the user-type design — the one part of it that was never built at all.
