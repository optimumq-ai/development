Why you might want this
The act already exists and is already built for the legal sections, so the page speaks one language instead of two. It produces a real record of who stood behind each decision, which is exactly what a city needs when a request is challenged a year later.
What it costs you
A formal confirmation means something on exemptions and appeals. Asking for the same ceremony on how long each task should take spends that weight on something trivial, and people who confirm twenty things stop reading the twenty-first. It also does not stop somebody confirming their own work.
What it needs from the role model
A rule for who may confirm what. That is the permission-group half of the user-type design — the part that says Senior Legal owns the legal rules and the System Administrator does not.
