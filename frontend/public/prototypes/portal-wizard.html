Item Search

Searching for records

Your next step is the record search. You can search for more than one type of record, but every search should be as specific as possible — one type of record, described narrowly. For example, "building permits issued in 2025 for Main Street" is one clear request. Business licenses would be a separate item.

The AI assistant will try to find matching records for you to review. Some record types — paper files, emails and texts, audio, video, and photos — aren't in the instant catalog and are searched by the Open Records team. You can still request them here; they just won't show instant results to pick from.

§2c
The assistant only ever gathers and refines one description, then runs the search and steps aside. Everything after a search is click-driven.
